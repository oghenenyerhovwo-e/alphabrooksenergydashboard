## Schema (`prisma/schema.prisma`)
- `enum UserRole { IT BUSINESS_DEVELOPMENT SALES OPERATIONS FINANCE MANAGEMENT DRIVER }` (ADMIN renamed to IT).
- New enums: `SaleSource {MANUAL ZOHO}`, `SaleStatus {PENDING_ALLOCATION ALLOCATED}`, `AuditAction {CREATE UPDATE DELETE}`, `AuditEntityType {OUTCOME_TARGET OUTCOME_ACHIEVEMENT SALE_RECORD SALE_ALLOCATION}`.
- `User`: `entraId String?` (now nullable), `username String? @unique`, `passwordHash String?`, `failedLoginCount Int @default(0)`, `lockedUntil DateTime?`, plus back-relations `driver`, `saleRecordsCreated/Updated`, `saleAllocations`, `saleAllocationsCreated/Updated`, `auditEntries`.
- `Driver`: `userId String? @unique`, `user User?` (onDelete SetNull).
- `OutcomeTarget`: `setByInstructionOf String?`, `instructionDate DateTime? @db.Date`.
- `SaleRecord`: product, source, zohoPaymentId (unique, nullable), invoiceNumber (not unique), customerName, zohoCustomerId, paymentDate (`@db.Date`), periodYear, periodMonth, totalQuantity `Decimal(20,3)`, unit, status, reversesSaleId (self-relation "SaleReversal", Restrict), notes, createdBy/updatedBy, timestamps.
- `SaleAllocation`: saleRecordId (Restrict), userId (Restrict), sharePercent `Decimal(5,2)`, quantity `Decimal(20,3)`, marginPerUnit `Decimal(18,4)`, generatedValue `Decimal(30,2)`, createdBy/updatedBy, timestamps; `@@unique([saleRecordId, userId])`.
- `AuditEntry` (append-only): entityType, entityId (no FK), parentEntityId, action, before/after `Json?`, actorUserId (Restrict), actorName, actorRole, reason, createdAt.
